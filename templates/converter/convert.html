      Built for privacy

      Personal converter defaults — not a public file dump.

      • Session-owned jobs. History and downloads stay tied to your browser session, not a shared feed.
      • Ephemeral files. Uploads and outputs expire after {{ job_ttl_hours }} hour{{ job_ttl_hours|pluralize }} and are purged.
      • No account required. Convert without signing up; nothing here pretends to keep your files forever.

      FAQ

      Straight answers for a public converter page.

      What formats can I convert?

      Only pairs registered in this build — shown in Supported conversions above. Some targets are marked best effort when quality or fidelity can vary.

      Can I convert multiple files at once?

      Yes. Upload up to {{ batch_max_files }} files to the same target format. Finished batches download as a ZIP.

      How long are my files kept?

      Jobs expire after {{ job_ttl_hours }} hour{{ job_ttl_hours|pluralize }}. After that, uploads and outputs are removed automatically.

      Is there a rate limit?

      Yes. Conversions are rate-limited per client so one browser cannot flood the queue. If you hit the cap, wait a bit and try again.
